Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know about commissions, payouts, taxes, and channel eligibility.

Do I need a GST number to join and earn?

No, a GST number is not required. As an individual creator, you only need a valid PAN card and an active Indian bank account for monthly direct transfers. (If you operate a registered company or firm with GST, you can optionally provide it during business onboarding).

When and how do I receive my creator payouts?

Payouts are transferred directly into your bank account on a predictable monthly cycle for all verified student purchases. You also get full visibility with detailed monthly statements tracking every coupon redemption and payout.

Can I apply if I only have a Telegram channel or WhatsApp group (no YouTube)?

Yes, absolutely! You do not need a YouTube channel. Many of Testbook's most active creator partners run focused Telegram exam channels, WhatsApp study groups, or Instagram education pages. Any genuine creator or mentor with an active student community is warmly welcome to apply.

Who is eligible to become a Testbook Creator?

Creators, faculty members, doubt mentors, subject experts, and digital content creators who share exam preparation content, test series, syllabus tips, or study materials can apply. Applications are reviewed within 24 to 48 hours.

How is my commission calculated?

Commissions are earned as a percentage of eligible student purchases (typically 15% to 20% on Testbook Pass One). Exact commission tiers are outlined in your creator welcome agreement.

How do I receive my personal creator coupon code?

Once your application is approved by our team (typically within 1–2 business days), you will receive your personalized coupon code (e.g., YOURNAME10) via call or email to start sharing right away.

Is there any fee to join the Creator Partner Program?

No. Applying and partnering is 100% free forever. Testbook will never ask you for any registration fee, security deposit, or subscription charge.
